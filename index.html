Crema

A coffee journal for iPhone. Log every cup, see today's caffeine poured into a glass, and find your favorite cafés on a map.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I log my usual with one touch?

On the Today tab, hold a card under "Your usuals" until it fills. A quick tap won't log a cup, so you can't add one by accident.

Scanning didn't fill in my drink.

Scanning reads the printed or written text on the cup or receipt. Make sure the label is in focus and well lit. It fills in only empty fields, so you can type or fix anything it missed.

How is caffeine calculated?

From typical amounts for each drink and size, which you can change on any cup. It's an estimate, not medical advice. Settings explains the method.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear on the Today tab) and choose "Export as CSV" to get a spreadsheet of every cup.

Does Crema sync between devices?

Not yet. Your log is stored only on your iPhone.

Privacy

Crema has no account and doesn't collect any data. Read the privacy policy.
