Crema

Crema estimates the caffeine still in your system and tells you the latest time each of your drinks still wears off before bed.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

What is a drink’s “last call”?

The latest time you can have that drink and still be under 50 mg of caffeine at your bedtime, a level generally considered low enough not to disturb sleep. It moves earlier with every drink you log.

Why does a drink say “Not tonight”?

Having it now would leave more than 50 mg in your system at bedtime. Tap it to see by how much, and when you would be under 50 mg instead.

How is caffeine calculated?

From typical amounts for each drink and size, which you can change on any drink. Crema assumes your body clears half of it every 4, 5, or 7 hours, depending on what you chose for “Coffee Lasts” in Settings. These are estimates, not medical advice.

How do I get the last-call reminder?

Turn on Last-Call Reminder in Settings (the gear on the Today tab). Crema sends one notification 30 minutes before the last call for your usual drink, on days when there’s still time.

Scanning didn’t fill in my drink.

Scanning reads the printed or written text on the cup or receipt. Make sure the label is in focus and well lit. It fills in only empty fields, so you can type or fix anything it missed.

Where did the map and spending go?

Crema 2.0 focuses on caffeine and sleep, so it no longer records prices or places. Drinks you logged with them keep them: they still show on each drink’s page and in the export.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. In Settings, under Your Data, choose Export as CSV to get a spreadsheet of every drink.

Does Crema sync between devices?

Not yet. Your log is stored only on your iPhone.

Privacy

Crema has no account and doesn’t collect any data. Read the privacy policy.
