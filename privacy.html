Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Crema is a coffee journal for iPhone made by Boyucheng Zhang. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it stays on your iPhone.

What Crema stores

The cups you log: the drink, and any details you choose to add, such as the shop, size, price, rating, notes, your own fields, a photo, and the place you bought it. Crema also keeps a few settings, such as your daily caffeine limit.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone. Crema has no account, no server, and no analytics, and the developer never receives any of your data.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo or scan a cup, Crema uses the camera or the photo you pick. Scanning reads the text on the cup with Apple's on-device Vision framework and, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Both run on your iPhone; the photo is not uploaded anywhere.

Location

Crema asks for your location only when you add a location to a cup, and it saves just that cup's coordinates on your iPhone. The Map tab shows those places using Apple Maps, which is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Siri and Shortcuts

"Log my usual" and "Caffeine today" run inside Crema on your iPhone. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and export

Your data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share a cup or export your log as a spreadsheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Delete any cup from its page, delete everything with Settings → Delete All Cups (Settings is the gear on the Today tab), or delete the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely. Device backups you make with iCloud or a computer may still contain a copy until they are replaced.

Tracking and advertising

Crema contains no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Crema does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Caffeine estimates

Caffeine amounts are estimates based on typical drinks. They are not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
