Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026, for Crema 2.0

Crema is an iPhone app for timing caffeine around sleep, made by Boyucheng Zhang. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it stays on your iPhone.

What Crema stores

The drinks you log: the drink, the time, its caffeine, and any details you choose to add, such as the shop, size, rating, a note, and a photo. Crema also keeps your settings: your bedtime, how long coffee lasts for you, your daily caffeine limit, and the drinks you picked during setup. Drinks logged with Crema 1.0 may also have a price, your own fields, and the place you bought them; Crema keeps those as they are.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone. Crema has no account, no server, and no analytics, and the developer never receives any of your data.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo of a cup or receipt, Crema uses the camera or the photo you pick. It reads the text with Apple’s on-device Vision framework and, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Both run on your iPhone; the photo is not uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the last-call reminder, Crema schedules notifications on your iPhone from what you’ve logged. They are created on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Siri and Shortcuts

“Log my usual” and “Can I still have coffee?” run inside Crema on your iPhone. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Export

Your data leaves your iPhone only when you export your log as a spreadsheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Delete any drink from its page, delete everything with Settings → Delete All Drinks (Settings is the gear on the Today tab), or delete the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely. Device backups you make with iCloud or a computer may still contain a copy until they are replaced.

Tracking and advertising

Crema contains no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Crema does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Caffeine estimates

Caffeine amounts, and how quickly caffeine wears off, are estimates based on typical drinks and an average person. They are not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
